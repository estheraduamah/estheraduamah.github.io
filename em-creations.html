E&M Creations is a collaborative design and fabrication venture built around bringing thoughtful, custom-crafted pieces from initial sketch to finished physical product.

Balancing aesthetic storytelling with hands-on prototyping, we iterate across material selection, digital design files, and small-batch production workflows tailored to individual clients and campus communities.

Running E&M Creations sharpened how I approach end-to-end product ownership—from listening closely to what makes a piece personally meaningful to managing timelines, sourcing, and repeatable quality.

01 /

Concept to Physical Artifact

Every piece begins with a collaborative design brief—translating a client's story, palette, or occasion into vector layouts, material tests, and finished tangible work.

02 /

Small-Batch Workflow & Craft Quality

We developed repeatable jigging, finishing, and packaging standards so custom commissions maintain consistent craft detail across every batch.
