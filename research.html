Research & Projects

TLDR

My primary work focuses on health equity, accessible care, and biomedical engineering. I bridge the gap between data and human stories, building systems that address systemic inequities and improve patient outcomes.

I have had the privilege of being a research assistant in three distinct labs: the Daub Lab at Karolinska Institutet, the Human-Centered Computing Lab at Olin College, and the Health Equity and Access Lab at Olin College. Across these roles, my work has ranged from conducting wet-lab research and developing ML approaches for spatial transcriptomics at Karolinska, to designing human-centered AI interfaces for older adults navigating patient portals, and analyzing clinical trial methodologies to propose targeted solutions for participant distrust.
